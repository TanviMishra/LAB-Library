Our R&D Process

prototypes

At the LAB at Rockwell Group, we have a robust R&D practice that explores new technologies and materials in service of building our creative practice, improving client facing work and nurturing curiosities. This library is a collection of some of our experiments.

Sketch Sessions

We begin with collaborative sketch sessions: quick drawings, references, and notes that align the team on intent, constraints, and a direction worth prototyping. The goal is shared clarity before time is spent in materials or software.

Prototyping

Prototypes translate ideas into something we can touch, light, or hear. We build rough models and technical tests to learn what works, what fails, and what should change—often in parallel across physical builds, software, and content.

Play Testing

Play testing puts work in front of people early. We observe pacing, comprehension, and delight, then fold feedback into the next iteration—treating the lab as a loop rather than a single handoff.

What If’s

“What If” explorations ask speculative questions without committing to production. They are a sandbox for visuals, interaction, and storytelling—often feeding later projects when the time and context are right.
